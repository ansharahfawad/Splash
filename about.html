Frequently Asked Questions

What age can my child start lessons?
Children can start as early as 6 months old in our Parent & Toddler class, with a parent in the water alongside them.
How long is each session?
Each class session runs 30-45 minutes depending on the age group and skill level.
What should my child bring?
A swimsuit, towel, and goggles (optional but recommended for older children).
Do you offer private lessons?
Yes, private one-on-one lessons are available upon request. Contact us for scheduling and pricing.
